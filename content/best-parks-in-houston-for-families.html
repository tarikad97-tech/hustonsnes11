The best park for your family depends on what your kids want to do. This guide compares four free-to-enter options by what each actually offers, based on the operators' own information.

Quick comparison

  • Water play: Hermann Park's Commons, Discovery Green
  • Rides (paid): Hermann Park train, carousel, pedal boats
  • Trails and nature: Houston Arboretum
  • Skyline views and nature play: Buffalo Bayou Park

Hermann Park

The Commons Play Garden and water play area is listed as open daily from 9 am to 9 pm. The railroad and carousel run 10 am to 6 pm and pedal boats 10 am to 5 pm; those rides need tickets, and hours can change with weather. The rides, cafes and play areas close on Thanksgiving and Christmas. The park itself is open 6 am to 11 pm, and free parking lots serve the zoo and Miller Outdoor Theatre areas. The McWilliams Dog Park is closed Mondays.

Discovery Green

Downtown, the park includes a water park for kids, a playground, a lake, dog runs and two restaurants. It is open daily 6 am to 11 pm, and admission is always free. Restrooms are by the Alkek Building near the playground. METRORail's Convention District stop is a short walk away.

Houston Arboretum & Nature Center

Choose this one for a quieter walk. Admission is free, with a parking fee except Thursdays and select Sundays. It has about 2 miles of accessible trails, and mobility devices can be borrowed free at the Nature Center. Trails are for hiking only, there are no designated picnic areas, dogs must be leashed, and plants and animals are protected, so kids shouldn't pick or catch anything.

Buffalo Bayou Park

KPRC describes a nature play area with a slide, climbing structures, water features and skyline views, next to the Jamail Skatepark, with free parking. We could not verify this against the park operator's current site, so check before you go.

Tips

  • Check the operator's site for water play closures before you leave.
  • Bring a change of clothes where there is water play.
