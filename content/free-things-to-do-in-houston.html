Houston has plenty of no-cost options, but "free" often comes with fine print: parking fees, paid add-ons, or days when a venue is closed. This guide sticks to what official sources say and flags the catches.

Free parks and public spaces

Discovery Green

This 12-acre downtown park is open daily from 6 am to 11 pm, and the park says admission is always free. It has a water park for kids, dog runs and a lake. Activities on the lake, such as kayaking, may carry separate fees, so check the park's site before you go.

Hermann Park

The 445-acre park near the Museum District is open daily 6 am to 11 pm, according to the Hermann Park Conservancy. Walking, picnicking and the grounds cost nothing. The train, carousel and pedal boats are ticketed. Free parking lots are listed on the Conservancy's page, and three METRORail stops are nearby.

Miller Outdoor Theatre

Inside Hermann Park, Miller Outdoor Theatre is listed by the Houston Museum District as always free. You can watch from the lawn (the Hill) or get a free ticket for covered seating. Performance times vary, so check the theatre's schedule.

Houston Arboretum & Nature Center

Admission to the nature center and trails is free. Parking is not always free: it is free on Thursdays and on select Sundays, and for members. Hours change seasonally, and trails are for hiking only.

Buffalo Bayou Park

KPRC reports that the nature play area next to the skatepark is free to visit. We haven't confirmed current facilities with the park operator, so verify before a trip.

Free museums

  • Menil Collection: admission is always free. Open Wednesday to Sunday, 11 am to 7 pm; closed Monday and Tuesday. Parking is free.
  • Museum of Fine Arts, Houston: permanent collections are free on Thursdays, courtesy of Shell USA, Inc. Special exhibitions may cost extra; check the museum.
  • Holocaust Museum Houston: free for everyone on Thursdays from 2 pm to 5 pm, and always free for ages 0 to 18.

Plan a free Thursday

Thursday lines up several offers: free MFAH permanent collections, free Holocaust Museum Houston admission from 2 to 5 pm, and free parking at the Arboretum. The Menil is also open on Thursdays. Details can change, so confirm each before you go.

Hidden costs to check

  • Parking at venues that charge it
  • Ride and boat tickets in parks
  • Special exhibitions
  • Closed days, especially Menil on Monday and Tuesday
