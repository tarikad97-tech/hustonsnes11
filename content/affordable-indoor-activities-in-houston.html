When it is too hot or wet to be outside, Houston's museums offer low-cost options. This guide covers three that publish clear free-admission policies. It is not a complete list yet; science and children's museums will be added once their current pricing is verified.

Menil Collection: always free

The Menil welcomes all visitors free of charge, with free parking. It is open Wednesday to Sunday, 11 am to 7 pm, and closed Monday and Tuesday. Photography is not allowed in the galleries, food and drink are not permitted in the museum buildings, and strollers pushed by an adult are welcome.

Museum of Fine Arts, Houston: free Thursdays

Admission to the permanent collections is free on Thursdays. Other free options listed by the museum: Texas Lone Star Card holders (including SNAP, WIC and TANF) can get free general admission for up to six people from the same household with a photo ID, and Bank of America's Museums on Us program gives cardholders one free admission. Special exhibitions may be priced separately.

Holocaust Museum Houston: free Thursday afternoons

Admission is free to everyone on Thursdays from 2 pm to 5 pm. Ages 0 to 18 and college students with valid ID are always free. The content is serious, so parents should consider whether it suits younger children.

A low-cost museum day

On a Thursday, you could visit the Menil, then MFAH, then the Holocaust Museum from 2 pm, all in the Museum District. Confirm hours and policies on each site first.
